Calibration record

There is no win rate here and no trading results — we do not have them at all. We publish one thing: are the risk boundaries printed in every report breached at the rate they claim? An honest model at 95% confidence is breached on ~5% of days only — no more (which would understate the risk) and no less (which would overstate the caution).

Every figure below is computed by a point-in-time historical sweep, with no forward look. Where the sample is too small for a reliable judgement (minimum 20), we say so rather than guess.
